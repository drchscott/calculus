Corollary 1.3.7.

  1. If \(q\) is a nonzero rational number and \(a\) is a nonzero number at which \(a^q\) is defined, then \(\displaystyle \lim_{x\to a}x^q=a^q\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. If \(p(x)\) and \(q(x)\) are polynomials and \(q(a)\ne 0\text{,}\) then \(\displaystyle \lim_{x\to a}(p(x)/q(x))=p(a)/q(a)\text{.}\)
